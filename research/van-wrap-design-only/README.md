## 4. Brief checklist (send this to whoever you hire)

1. Exact van: year, make, model, wheelbase, roof height, door and window layout.
2. Photos of all sides, plus measurements of any trim or obstacles.
3. Coverage: full, ¾, partial, or lettering only. Is the roof included?
4. Brand assets: vector logo, colors (Pantone or CMYK), fonts.
5. Message: business name, services, phone, website, QR code (keep it readable from about 50 ft).
6. Deliverables: print-ready vector files (AI/PDF), CMYK, to scale, 3–6 in bleed, fonts outlined, images at full resolution.
7. Mockups of every side, plus the number of revisions and the deadline.
8. Before final export, check file format and bleed with the shop that will print it.
9. **Written transfer of copyright, with all source files and licensed fonts and stock images.**
